## What changed (user's manual edit)

The over-balance badge (wallet icon) now uses a new `card.overby` key ("OVER BY $X") instead of reusing `card.over` ("OVER $X"), which stays on the over-limit badge (speedometer icon) unchanged. `en.ts`/`fr.ts` both got the new key ("OVER BY" / "DÉPASSÉ DE"). Also a harmless import-ordering/formatting cleanup at the top of `BentoCard.tsx` (no behavior change).

## Review verdict: good change

Yesterday's balance-vs-limit disambiguation fix ([[2026-08-30d-session]]) gave both badges identical `"OVER $X"` wording, but `$X` meant different things per badge: for the limit badge, `$X` is the limit itself (you're over your $50 limit — correct to show as-is); for the balance badge, `$X` was `Math.abs(remaining)`, i.e. the *overspend amount*, not the balance. So "OVER $70.00" on the balance badge read as if $70 were the balance, when it was actually how far past it you were. This manual fix disambiguates by giving the balance badge its own "OVER BY $X" wording, which correctly frames the number as an overage amount. Icons still carry the wallet-vs-speedometer distinction on top of this.

## Fixed to keep it green

The new wording broke 2 pre-existing assertions in `components/__tests__/BentoCard.test.tsx` that still expected the old shared `"OVER $20.00"` text for the balance case. Updated both to `"OVER BY $20.00"`.
