ChronoBudget is a calm, offline-first expense tracker built for phones with dark, OLED-friendly screens.

Track every expense into one of three simple categories — Needs, Wants, and Savings — and set a monthly limit for each. No spreadsheets, no complicated budgeting methodology, no account required.

FEATURES

- Fast entry: type "15 coffee" and ChronoBudget figures out the category and subcategory automatically, learning from your corrections over time.
- Savings goals (sinking funds): earmark money toward a named goal — a car repair fund, a vacation, a big purchase — and watch it fill up as you contribute.
- Budget limits with live progress bars, so you always know how close you are to your monthly limit.
- Recurring transactions: bills and subscriptions post automatically on the schedule you set.
- Multiple accounts: track where your money actually sits (checking, cash, savings) and move money between them.
- History and trends: review past spending and see a 6-month breakdown by category.
- CSV export/import: your data is always yours to take with you.
- Fully offline: no account, no login, no backend, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your device.
- Available in English and Français, with more languages planned.

ChronoBudget doesn't try to be a full accounting suite. It's built to answer one question quickly, every day: is my spending on track?
